🔤 English 1st Paper (Syllabus & Marks Distribution - 2026)

📊 Marks Distribution (Total Marks: 100 | Time: 3 Hours)

  • Reading Part: 60 Marks (Seen Passage 1 & 2, Unseen Passage, Matching, Rearrange)
  • Writing Part: 40 Marks (Paragraph, Story, Graph/Chart, Letter/Email)
🌱 Half-Yearly Examination
  • EFT Book: Units 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5.
  • Writing: Descriptive Paragraphs, Informal Letters, Story Writing.
🌻 Annual Examination
  • EFT Book: Units 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12.
  • Writing: Graph & Chart analysis, Formal Emails, Dialogue/Completing Story.

✍️ English 2nd Paper (Syllabus & Marks Distribution - 2026)

📊 Marks Distribution (Total Marks: 100 | Time: 3 Hours)

  • Grammar Items: 60 Marks (Gap filling, Transformation, Tag Questions, Connectors, Punctuation)
  • Composition Part: 40 Marks (CV writing, Formal letters, Paragraphs)
🌱 Half-Yearly Examination
  • Grammar: Articles, Prepositions, Right forms of Verbs, Changing Sentences.
  • Writing: CV with Cover Letter, Notice & Complaint Letters.
🌻 Annual Examination
  • Grammar: Tag Questions, Sentence Connectors, Narration, Punctuation Marks.
  • Writing: Cause & Effect Paragraphs, Formal Applications.
